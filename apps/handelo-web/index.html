Who does what

Built for people who want to understand and manage tokenized-stock opportunities without handing an AI chat interface unrestricted trading power.

AI explains

Price gaps, market state and what matters now, in plain language. The model reads the market. It does not hold the wallet.

The system governs

You decide

Intelligence is separated from authority.

What the AI can do

  • Interpret your request and identify the intended action
  • Resolve the relevant market information
  • Explain the opportunity before anything executes

What the AI cannot do

  • Hold a private key or control funds
  • Move money directly
  • Skip rules, approval or the wallet boundary

Every AI-generated instruction passes through deterministic application logic and the configured execution gates before it can reach the wallet provider.

Rules and policy

Deterministic constraints decide what is safe, not the model: spending limits, market state, reference price gaps, portfolio exposure and minimum reserve.

User approval

Approval is an explicit boundary. A review returns PASS, REVIEW or BLOCK, with reasons instead of a bare green or red light.

Scoped wallet

Your wallet and the agent wallet are separate. A session is scoped by chain, permissions, spending limits, allowed assets, reserve, expiry and revocation. Requests outside it are rejected.

Audit trail

Strategy execution state is recorded across trigger, risk check, execution, verification, completion or failure, and retry and recovery.

01 / Why we built Handelo

Markets close. Tokenized stocks do not.

Traditional market references can stop moving while their onchain representations continue trading. That creates a decision problem: a user needs the token price, the underlying reference, the size of the gap, market state, portfolio exposure and execution conditions in the same decision path.

02 / The problem

A tokenized-stock trade carries context.

A buy button alone does not explain whether the market is open, whether the token is trading away from its reference, whether the order changes portfolio risk, whether wallet authority is sufficient, or whether the execution should actually be allowed.

03 / The solution

Understand. Strategize. Execute.

Handelo connects market intelligence, AI explanation, strategy construction, deterministic policy, portfolio risk, wallet permissions, execution and verification into one governed workflow: Discover → Understand → Strategize → Check Risk → Review → Approve → Execute → Monitor.

04 / The design principle

Intelligence is separated from authority.

The AI can interpret intent and explain the market, but it is not the signing boundary. Private keys stay outside the LLM and browser UI. Deterministic application logic, policy, wallet scope, approval and verification remain authoritative.

What Handelo is responsible for

  • Resolve tokenized-stock market context from Binance-backed BSC data.
  • Compare the onchain token representation with its underlying reference.
  • Turn natural-language intent into a structured strategy or transaction path.
  • Apply deterministic policy and portfolio-risk checks before execution.
  • Keep wallet authority scoped and separate from the AI reasoning layer.
  • Record execution lifecycle and verification state rather than assuming success.

What Handelo refuses to pretend

  • Additional tokenized stocks are not called supported until they are individually validated.
  • Passing tests or mocks is not presented as proof of live execution.
  • A wallet connection is not treated as unrestricted spending authority.
  • A generated or client-side transaction hash is not treated as proof of settlement.
  • P&L is not inferred from current balances when trustworthy cost basis is unavailable.
  • Unverified wallet, security or provider behavior remains a blocking condition.

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Handelo a chatbot?

No. The AI is an intelligence and command layer inside a broader system of market data, strategy, portfolio, policy, wallet, execution and verification.

Can the AI directly move my money?

No. The model never receives private keys and does not control the wallet. Execution passes through deterministic application logic and the wallet provider boundary.

Does Handelo hold my private key?

The intended architecture does not require a private key or seed phrase. Handelo works with wallet context and a scoped agent wallet at the provider boundary.

What stock does Handelo support today?

NVIDIA (NVDA), represented by the bStock token NVDAB on BNB Chain. It is the only asset validated end to end.

Does it support every bStock Binance returns?

Not yet as a validated claim. Ingestion is provider driven and can consume Binance BNB Chain RWA assets, but each additional asset must be validated before it is presented as supported.

What happens if a risk check fails?

The transaction is blocked. The review shows the reasons, so you can see what prevented execution.

Does passing the AI review mean a trade will happen?

No. Policy, portfolio risk, wallet authorization, security checks, quote freshness, explicit confirmation, execution configuration and verification all still apply.

Is Handelo live with real-money autonomous trading?

No. Substantial engineering and local validation is complete. Controlled real-money validation and the remaining live provider checks are later gates.
